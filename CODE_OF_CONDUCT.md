# Code of Conduct

Exposure Radar welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and credentials.
- Keep discussions relevant to building and maintaining the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behaviour harms the community. Report a
conduct problem privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not
disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporter privacy where
possible, and apply proportionate action.
